# C5: UE fTetWild contact mesh integration

## Decision (2026-10-08)

User clarified that no allowed volume-shrinkage value should be introduced. Keep
the current volume-preserving runtime constraints and material settings. This
change targets simulation mesh topology and its data transfer, not tissue bulk
modulus calibration. Numerical volume error is still measured and is not a
biological compressibility allowance.

## TetWild versus fTetWild

The previous experiment's `tetwild15` and `tetwild10` labels refer to the UE wrapper,
not the original 2018 TetWild executable. The installed UE 5.8 source file
`GeometryAlgorithms/Private/FTetWildWrapper.cpp` includes
`ThirdParty/fTetWild/FloatTetwild.h` and calls `floatTetWild`.
Thus the tested native implementation ALREADY IS fTetWild.

The 2020 fTetWild paper replaces rational triangle insertion with an incremental
floating-point approach, aims for similar output quality at lower construction
cost, and preserves validity in floating-point coordinates. It trades away the
formal guarantee of inserting every input triangle. Neither mesher itself is a
soft-body material law or a volume conservation solver.

Use the UE-integrated implementation, avoiding an extra executable/dependency and
an untested binary ABI. This is not a claim that UE's bundled revision is identical
to the latest upstream release, nor a fresh speed benchmark of the original TetWild.

References:
- https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.03581
- https://github.com/wildmeshing/fTetWild
- Original: https://github.com/Yixin-Hu/TetWild

## Compressibility research and its limits

There is no defensible universal percentage inferred merely from breast anatomy.
Large axial deformation is not equal to large volume loss. Fluid-rich tissue can
change shape easily while remaining approximately volume preserving.

- Goodbrake et al., 2022, *On the three-dimensional mechanical behavior of human
  breast tissue*, DOI 10.1007/s10439-022-02951-y: experiments and inverse fitting
  emphasize anisotropy and support effectively incompressible models in their
  tested conditions. This does not define an allowed whole-breast volume-loss cap.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10116697/
- *An analysis of the mechanical parameters used for finite element compression
  of a high-resolution 3D breast phantom* (2011) uses nu=.49 partly for numerical
  stability. That number is a modeling choice, not a measured universal shrinkage.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3203130/
- Pacheco et al. (2025), DOI 10.1002/mp.17980, uses nu=.4995 as a fixed internal
  parameter while optimizing other tissue parameters. Again not a loss percentage.
  https://aapm.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/mp.17980
- Sheen et al. 2021 separates zonal volume preservation and local compression,
  addressing locking problems with high bulk resistance on coarse meshes.
  https://elrnv.com/projects/volume-preserving-simulation-of-soft-tissue-with-skin/

For an isotropic small-strain material K=E/[3(1-2nu)] and hydrostatic fractional
volume change is approximately -p/K. It depends on pressure and material assumptions;
nu alone cannot be converted into a universal allowed loss. The existing convex
contact proxy is also larger than a segmented anatomical tissue domain. Treating
its percentage volume change as a direct physiological measurement is inappropriate.

## Integration

`UVamBreastContactBuilder::Build` can finish with `RemeshFTetWild` before surface
refinement and GPU deformer generation. Existing source evidence and its transfer
cage are retained as an authoring interpolation domain; final simulation cells
come from fTetWild, not the radial center. Each side is meshed independently.

- Parameters: relative edge .15, envelope tolerance .001, max iterations 40.
- Limits: <=4096 points / <=20000 tets per side; no cell volume <=1e-8 cm3;
  minimum mean-ratio .05; initial envelope volume deviation <=1%.
- Positive-volume orientation, deterministically ordered extracted boundary faces.
- Source attachment weights, root/nipple evidence, and all Shape Morph deltas are
  interpolated through nonnegative source tetrahedral/closest-face coordinates.
- Kinematic source support threshold .95; runtime dynamic state stays per instance.
- Original skin positions are rebound with positive coordinates plus detail offsets;
  >1mm outside the new cage rejects construction instead of silently extrapolating.
- The established render refinement and GPU residual deformation path are reused.
- Build identity: `breast-contact-c5-ue-ftetwild-v1` (render suffix added later).
  Schema remains 4 because serialized fields and runtime interpretation are unchanged.
- Old committed assets are not remeshed or overwritten. New runtime outputs are
  generated by the normal character build pipeline.

Material coefficients, rest-volume target, skin bending, nipple preservation and
Jiggle parameters are unchanged. Runtime contact integration also needs adaptation:
initialize all Chaos proxies without collision sources, then install the coupled
constraints before exposing the first real contact. Otherwise a newly overlapping
world collider can invert cells during the unprotected first solve.

The original collision projection permitted severe local cell compression even
when total volume was accurate. Experiments are retained under Saved/ContactFTetWild:
`rejected-unbounded.log`, `rejected-global-guard.log`, `rejected-twelve-sweeps.log`,
and `rejected-facefloor.log`. A global feasible-iterate rollback was rejected:
it can freeze deformation and leave penetration. No test thresholds were relaxed.
Current candidate instead backtracks each surface-contact projection against its
incident cell volumes, uses additional coupled local sweeps, and a 1 mm collision
thickness for the fTetWild/render embedding. The final engineering checks pass;
these settings are numerical contact treatment, not biological calibration.

## Final experiment status (2026-10-08)

The new backend is integrated as an opt-in editor build path:
`vam.Contact.BuildFTetWild 1` before generating a new character. Default is 0;
`vam.Contact.BuildFTetWild 0` restores the existing build backend. This selection
only affects newly built profiles. Existing committed assets are not mutated.
Runtime dispatch comes from the immutable profile build identity.

- Isolated Editor build succeeded. New character build, independent reload and
  verification succeeded. Profile validation also checks Morph particle counts.
- Candidate BP: `/Game/VamRuntime/R_619448199d803edf1ab83af9/BP_VamCharacter`.
  Contact profile: same folder, `DA_BreastContact`.
- 1380 particles / 5337 cells; 39 left and 48 right kinematic supports.
  Minimum mean-ratio quality 0.344698, maximum render rebind offset 0.019777 cm.
- ContactBulk, ContactRenderCurve and GPU ContactRuntime all succeeded.
- Reported pressure volume error <=0.21%; no inverted cells; minimum J 0.2000.
  Actual world sphere render-vertex penetration 0.167375 cm (test limit 0.2 cm).
  The numerical error is not a configured physical volume-loss allowance.
- Local solve uses 4..24 sweeps with a cell-support early exit; contact projection
  does not deliberately lower a supported incident cell below J=0.2.
  Debug contact onset accounts for the 1 mm collision envelope, preserving the
  zero-pressure GPU/native reconstruction check.
- `Saved/ContactFTetWild/compare.html` and `visual/glass-press.mp4` contain real UE
  captures. Fine contact creases remain (especially the sharp platen). Improved
  tetrahedral quality alone has not resolved the visible surface issue.
- Solve cost remains high; see CPU timings in `results.json`. This is NOT a
  production performance acceptance. The experimental plugin binaries are not
  installed over the currently usable version.
- The test sphere is a kinematic rigid mesh, not proof of two-way force exchange.
  Anatomical realism, self/contact pairs and physiological calibration are not
  established by these tests. No visual acceptance is claimed.

Final compatibility check: the previously accepted C4 output
`R_05393c71101a04b1d7f0ed32` passes ContactBulk, ContactRenderCurve and GPU
ContactRuntime with the final binary. Initialization adaptation and new contact
settings are selected only for fTetWild profiles. Final Editor build succeeded;
no Game/cook pass or main-plugin installation was performed in this experiment.
Measured C5 contact CPU diagnostics in this run span about 39--47 ms; idle reports
zero solvers. These timings are from the automation host, not a packaged-game
benchmark. Source remains uncommitted on feature/chaos/breasts at HEAD 18bd300.
